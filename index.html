DIRECTOR'S WELCOME

Welcome from Our Director

Hello, I’m Kassandra Sheriff, Director of the Network for Children in Need Orphanage Home. For the past two years, I have had the profound honor of continuing the extraordinary legacy started by my late mother. She dedicated over 34 years of her life to loving and nurturing vulnerable children. After her passing, I felt a deep calling to carry forward her vision a responsibility I embrace with my whole heart.

With a background in business and strong skills in management and organization, I bring both professional expertise and personal passion to this role. In the past two years, I have worked tirelessly to ensure that every child in our care receives not only the love and support they need, but also meaningful opportunities to grow, learn, and thrive.

I am driven by a simple but powerful belief: every child deserves love, dignity, and a real chance to succeed regardless of their circumstances. It is both an honor and a calling to continue my mother’s life’s work.

Thank you for visiting us. We are truly grateful you are here.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, nurturing, and supportive environment for orphaned and vulnerable children, ensuring they receive quality care, education, and opportunities to grow into responsible and empowered individuals. We are committed to continuing the legacy of compassion and service by uplifting the less privileged and giving them hope for a brighter future.

Key Program Highlights

Education Support

Providing access to formal schooling, tutoring, and literacy programs to ensure every child has the foundation for a brighter future.

Health & Nutrition

Offering regular medical check‑ups, counseling, and balanced meals to promote healthy growth and wellbeing.

Emmergency, Care & Shelter

Ensuring immediate protection and safe housing for children in crisis situations.

Family Reunification

Working to reconnect children with extended family when safe and possible, to restore bonds and belonging.
