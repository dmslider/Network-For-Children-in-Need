Frequently Asked Questions

How can I make a donation?

You can donate through our website by clicking the "Donate Now" button. We accept credit cards, bank transfers, and other payment methods. All donations are secure and tax-deductible.

Can I sponsor a child?

Yes! We offer child sponsorship programs. For more information, please contact us at sponsor@networkforchildren.org or call our office during business hours.

How can I volunteer?

We welcome volunteers! Please visit our office or send us an email with information about your skills and availability. We have various volunteer opportunities available.

What does my donation support?

Your donation supports food, shelter, education, healthcare, clothing, and recreational activities for the children in our care. We provide detailed reports on how funds are used.

Is your organization registered?

Yes, we are a registered non-profit organization with all necessary certifications and clearances. We are committed to transparency and accountability in all our operations.

How can I stay updated?

Subscribe to our newsletter on the blog page, follow us on social media, or check our website regularly for updates on programs, events, and children's success stories.
