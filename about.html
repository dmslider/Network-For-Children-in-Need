About Us

Our Journey of Care and Compassion

Our Story

Network for Children in Need was founded with a simple yet powerful belief: every child deserves a loving home, quality education, and the opportunity to build a better future. For over 15 years, we have been dedicated to providing comprehensive care to children in need.

Our orphanage is more than just a shelter; it's a home filled with compassion, learning, and hope. We believe that with proper care, education, and support, every child can overcome their circumstances and achieve their full potential.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, nurturing environment where vulnerable children can grow, learn, and thrive. We are committed to:

  • Providing shelter, food, and clothing
  • Ensuring access to quality education
  • Offering healthcare and wellness services
  • Developing life skills and confidence
  • Creating a family-like environment
  • Preparing children for independent futures

Our Vision

A world where no child is left behind, where every child has access to love, care, education, and opportunity. We dream of a future where our children can lead independent, fulfilling lives and contribute positively to their communities.
