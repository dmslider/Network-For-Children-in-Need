About Us

Our Journey of Care and Compassion

Our Story

The Network for Children in Need Orphanage Home was founded by my late mother, who devoted more than 34 years of her life to serving vulnerable children with compassion, dignity, and unwavering dedication. Her vision created a safe haven where children could heal, grow, and dream of a brighter future.

After her passing, we felt deeply compelled to continue her remarkable legacy. With a professional background in business and valuable expertise in management and organization, I stepped into the role of Director two years ago.

These past two years have been a journey of learning, growth, and deep fulfillment. Drawing on both my academic training and hands-on experience,We have focused on strengthening the operations of the orphanage while ensuring that every child receives quality care and meaningful opportunities for personal development. What continues to inspire me every day is my passion for supporting the less privileged. I firmly believe that every child no matter their background deserves love, dignity, and the chance to thrive. Carrying forward my mother’s vision is not just a responsibility; it is a calling that fills me with purpose and hope. Today, we remain committed to honoring her legacy by creating a nurturing environment where children feel truly seen, valued, and empowered to build successful futures.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, nurturing, and supportive environment for orphaned and vulnerable children, ensuring they receive quality care, education, and opportunities to grow into responsible and empowered individuals. We are committed to continuing the legacy of compassion and service by uplifting the less privileged and giving them hope for a brighter future.

  • Providing shelter, food, and clothing
  • Ensuring access to quality education
  • Offering healthcare and wellness services
  • Developing life skills and confidence
  • Creating a family-like environment
  • Preparing children for independent futures

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a society where every child, regardless of background, has access to love, dignity, and equal opportunities. We aspire to become a model orphanage home that transforms lives, inspires communities, and creates future leaders who contribute positively to the world.
